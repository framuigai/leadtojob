  • Associations, not causes. {{ association_caveat }}
  • No validated score bands. {{ bands_note }}
  • No single satisfaction score. The three Overall Satisfaction subdomains are reported separately because they measure related but different things: an employee may consider the job a good fit and still experience frequent frustration.
  • Perception, not objective fact. {{ perception_caveat }}
  • No individual is visible. Responses are anonymous and pooled. Groups of fewer than {{ min_reporting_n }} are suppressed, and where one group is suppressed another is suppressed with it so the hidden figure cannot be recovered by subtracting the rest from the total.
  • Not for decisions about individuals. Nothing in this report may be used in an appraisal, a performance process, a promotion decision or any other decision about a particular employee.
  • Method. Domain scores are {{ scoring_method_label }}. "Not applicable" answers are excluded from the score entirely and reported separately as coverage; they are not counted as a middle answer.
