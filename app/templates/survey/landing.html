What we can and cannot promise

{# THE ANONYMITY CLAIM, STATED HONESTLY FOR THE DELIVERY MODE. Specification 9.2: "avoid promising absolute anonymity if the administration method cannot guarantee it."     {% if deployment.delivery_mode == 'open_link' %}
  • Your answers are not linked to you. This survey asks for no name, no email address and no employee number, and the system has nowhere to store one.
  • We cannot strictly prevent duplicate submissions. Everyone receives the same link, so we have no way to tell whether one person answered twice. We are telling you this rather than claiming something we cannot deliver.
    • {% else %}
  • One response per code. Your code can be used once.
  • We do not know which code is yours. The system stores only a one-way fingerprint of each code and never a record of who received it.
    • {% endif %} {# THE REPORTING PROMISE FOLLOWS THE THRESHOLD IN FORCE. `promise` comes from reporting_promise() in app/survey/routes.py. The same discipline that gives open_link and ticketed different anonymity wording applies here: a form claiming group-only reporting while the threshold is 1 is a false statement to someone being asked to answer candidly about their employer. tests assert the match. #} {% if promise == 'group' %}
  • Results are only ever reported for groups. Any group smaller than {{ cycle.min_reporting_n }} people is suppressed, and the next-smallest group with it, so a suppressed figure cannot be worked out by subtraction.
  • No individual response is shown to anyone - not to your manager, not to HR, not in any report or export. Managers receive group results for their team and nothing else.
    • {% elif promise == 'pair' %}
  • Results are reported for groups of two or more. We will not show a figure that rests on a single response. You should know that in a group of exactly two, the other person could work out your answers from the pair's average and their own.
  • No individual response is shown as such in any report or export.
    • {% else %}
  • Please read this before answering. This survey is currently set up to report a result even when only one person in a group has responded. That means a figure shown for your team may be based on your answers alone, and someone who knows who is in your team may be able to work out that it is yours.
  • We are telling you this rather than claiming your answers are grouped with other people's, because for now they may not be.
  • Answer only what you are comfortable putting your name to, and leave anything else blank or mark it as not applicable. A blank answer is left out of the results; it is not counted against you or treated as a middle answer.
    • {% endif %}
  • Answering honestly carries no consequence. Your responses are not used in any decision about you as an individual.
  • In the interest of being complete: like any website, the servers that carry this page keep ordinary technical logs, which include the network address you connect from. Those logs are separate from your answers and cannot be joined to them. We would rather say so than claim perfect anonymity.
