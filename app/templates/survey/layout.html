{# THE RESPONDENT-FACING LAYOUT. It deliberately does NOT extend base.html. No navbar, no leadTo wordmark, no admin shell, no product branding of any kind. {# DOUBLE-CLICK GUARD. Inline and first-party: the no-external-scripts rule in the comment above is about third-party requests from this page, and this makes none. SECONDARY BY DESIGN. The real guarantee is server-side — answers are written under uq_response_identity and the response counter sits behind `submitted_at`, so a re-sent POST cannot duplicate anything, and review() answers a retry with the same success response. A client or middlebox re-sending below the browser's form layer never runs this at all.
