Not proof of inattention — a genuinely uniform experience exists, and a small team that agrees is not a data problem. A rate that climbs across waves is the signal worth acting on: it suggests the instrument is too long or too repetitive.

What this page cannot tell you. The specification’s validation process also asks whether employees understand the questions consistently, whether any are repetitive or difficult to answer, and whether the survey works across different roles and settings. Those are answered by a structured pilot with respondent feedback — run a wave with pilot mode enabled — not by coefficients. Until that is done, the product note stands: this is a proposed instrument, not a validated one.
