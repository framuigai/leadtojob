No segments.

Two rules, both about protecting respondents. Results are reported one segment at a time: crossing two of them (department by length of service) narrows groups to a handful of people very fast. And options are a fixed list rather than free text - otherwise three spellings of one department become three groups, none of which reaches the reporting threshold, and the data is not wrong so much as gone.

Segments freeze on the first response of the first wave that uses them. After that, renaming an option would silently re-group answers given under the old meaning.
